Quality Pathways

Our Inspection Process

How we inspect, measure, log, and report deficiencies.

1

Book Inspection

Inquire through our online scheduler form. Select your property type, size, location, builder name, and preferred scheduling date.

2

Inspector Assigned

An engineering lead checks your property specifications and forms an audit checklist tailored to your builder's drawings.

3

Site Visit

Our civil engineers carry out a detailed on-site assessment. We bring advanced diagnostics tools directly to the building.

4

150+ Quality Checks

We run our 150+ point snag checklist, reviewing electrical wiring, water flow plumbing, brick works, framing, tile hollows, and alignments.

5

Photo Documentation

We capture high-resolution photos and thermal scans of every defect found, providing clear evidence for the developer.

6

Detailed Report

Our engineering managers compile findings into a clean, digital PDF report with severity categories, delivered in 24 hours.

7

Expert Consultation

Review the snag list via a one-on-one consultation call with our inspector to formulate repair requests for the builder.
