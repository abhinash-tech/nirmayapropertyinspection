Engineering Audits

Inspection Services

Unbiased civil engineering reviews for Hyderabad property buyers.

Home Inspection

A complete 150+ point audit of independent homes. We inspect floor levels, ceilings, brickworks, and safety clearances.

Apartment Inspection

Verification of plumbing shafts, balcony alignments, electrical panels, and builders' finishing standards for apartments.

Villa Inspection

Evaluating luxury properties, checking terrace waterproof systems, retaining walls, facade cladding, and individual overhead tanks.

Commercial Inspection

Specialized audits of offices, retail zones, and distribution warehouses. Checking fire systems, layouts, and HVAC assets.

Pre-Handover Inspection

A systematic snagging audit before receiving registry keys, giving buyers the leverage to request developer repairs.

New Construction Inspection

Phase-wise checks (foundation, slab structure, masonry, and plastering) to ensure structural standards are met throughout construction.

Electrical Inspection

Checking grounding loops, breaker panels, socket polarity, switch ratings, and potential fire risk hotspots.

Structural Inspection

Evaluating concrete beams, pillar joints, columns, slab shifts, wall cracks, and load-bearing parameters.

Plumbing Inspection

Checking line pressure, pipeline connections, traps, drainage slopes, and fixture seal integrity.

Water Leakage Inspection

Identifying damp spots and moisture paths in bathrooms, kitchens, and outer walls using specialized moisture meters.

Roof Inspection

Checking roof waterproofing, screed slopes, rainwater drain outlets, parapet joints, and solar mounts.

Thermal Imaging Inspection

Non-destructive thermography scans showing active moisture paths, electrical resistance overloads, and insulation cracks.
