Apartment Inspection

Non-Destructive Testing Methods

Unlike average local contractors, our engineers never guess. We utilize modern diagnostic apparatus to assess properties:

  • Infrared Thermography: Scanning wall spaces and piping for thermal variations indicating dampness or heat anomalies.
  • Digital Moisture Analysis: Evaluating core moisture percentages inside finishes and wall plaster without drills.
  • Laser Distance & Alignment Levelers: Measuring structural slants, flooring gaps, and framing deviations accurately.

Property Inspection FAQs

Have questions about inspection schedules, diagnostics tools, or snags? Find detailed answers below.

A property inspection snag list is a comprehensive catalog of defects, unfinished construction works, deviations from standard drawings, and quality concerns found in a property. NIRMAYA reports separate snags into Major, Moderate, and Minor categories with visual evidence.
A Pre-Handover Inspection provides you with visual proof of defects before taking registry possession. This ensures the builder is contractually responsible for snag rectifications before final keys are exchanged.
A typical residential home inspection takes between 3 to 5 hours depending on size. The detailed digital report is compiled, reviewed by an engineering manager, and delivered to your email inbox within 24 hours of site assessment.
