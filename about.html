About NIRMAYA

Redefining Inspection Standards

At NIRMAYA Property Inspection, we are redefining the standards of property audits, waterproofing solutions, and quality consultations across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Our journey began with expert property audits, helping homeowners, businesses, and developers identify critical construction defects, moisture leaks, and structural snags before they become costly problems. We bring deep engineering expertise, having inspected thousands of spaces using well-established diagnostic systems that set a new benchmark in India.

Over time, we expanded our core expertise into advanced water seepage and dampness investigations, ensuring long-lasting protection against structural deterioration. Today, we are trusted by homeowners, real estate developers, interior designers, and corporate partners for reliable, cost-effective, and independent solutions.

Backed by a highly qualified team of certified B.Tech & M.Tech civil engineers and industry experts, we deliver completely unbiased, independent third-party audits with state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment.

Our Mission

NIRMAYA Property Inspection excels in Property Audits, Water Seepage Diagnostics, and Independent Quality Inspections across South Asia. With expert solutions and a customer-first approach, we ensure durability, safety, and efficiency in every space.

Our Vision

NIRMAYA Property Inspection aims to lead in Property Audits, Waterproofing Consultations, and Independent Quality Audits. Committed to quality and innovation, we ensure defect-free, functional, and lasting spaces across residential, commercial, and hospitality sectors.

Advanced Seepage Diagnostics

Leakage & Dampness - The Silent Destroyers

Dampness and structural moisture are the leading causes of building deterioration. NIRMAYA provides precise scientific mapping to protect your property investment.

Without NIRMAYA Diagnostics

  • High Cost

    Uncertain repairs are proposed by contractors based on guess-work, leading to bloated bills and repeated budgets.

  • Destructive

    Contractors break walls, slabs, and tiles searching for the leak source, causing avoidable damage to your property structure.

  • Short Term

    Waterproofing without identifying the root cause results in dampness returning within 6–12 months, triggering mold growth.

With NIRMAYA Diagnostics

  • Cost-Cutting

    We provide precise locations of the leak, allowing targeted local repairs instead of rebuilding entire bathrooms or terraces.

  • Non-Invasive

    We locate dampness pathing and source points using thermal infrared scanners and concrete moisture meters without breaking anything.

  • Long Term

    We diagnose moisture travel paths so you can apply permanent waterproofing barriers using top-grade products (like MYK Arment).
